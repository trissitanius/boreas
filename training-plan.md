## Glossary of my cues
- **Proud chest:** chest up and out.
- **Banana:** squeeze the shoulder blades together, as if holding a banana between them.
- **Ronaldo:** lift the back off the pad and open the chest, like a "Siuuu" celebration.
- **Elbows to your pockets:** pull the elbows down toward your trouser pockets.
